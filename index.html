The studio

What we care about

ScoutVela Games is a small independent team. We build every game ourselves and keep improving it after launch, based on what players tell us.

Fair and free

All our games are free. Ads keep them free, and you never have to pay to finish a level.

Respect for privacy

We never ask for your name, email or phone number. Online features use an anonymous account you can delete in the game.

Always updated

New levels, worlds and features arrive in regular updates. Tell us what you want to see next.
