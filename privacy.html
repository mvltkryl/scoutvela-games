Privacy Policy

ScoutVela Games · Last updated 2 November 2026

This policy covers our games on Android (Google Play) and iPhone (App Store): Donut Destroyer, Strategic Rescue Operation, Block Isles, Crack the Rule, Legion Rush and Starword Atlas. It explains what data each game uses, why, and how you can delete it.

Donut Destroyer Strategic Rescue Operation Block Isles Crack the Rule Legion Rush Starword Atlas Advertising Contact

Donut Destroyer

Android package and iOS bundle ID: com.MBAGameOffice.StandalonePairing

Stored on your device

Your level progress, coins, lives, boosters and settings stay on your phone. We do not send them to a server.

Online features (teams, chat, friends)

If you use teams, chat, friends or life sending, the game creates an anonymous account for you. We store a random player ID and friend code, the player name you choose and your level, your team membership, friends and lives sent or received, and the messages you write in team chat and any reports you file. We never ask for your email, phone number or real name. This data is hosted by Supabase on servers in the European Union (Stockholm) and is encrypted in transit.

Chat safety

Chat messages are filtered for offensive words. You can report or block any player from their profile in the game. We review reports and remove players who break the rules.

Deleting your data

Tap Profile → Delete my account to delete your online account, team membership, friendships and life gifts at once. Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your phone.

Strategic Rescue Operation

Android package and iOS bundle ID: com.MBAGameOffice.StrategicRescueOperation

Stored on your device

Your level progress, coins, hero skins, castle and settings stay on your phone. The game has no account, no chat and no server of its own. Uninstalling the game removes all of it.

Block Isles

Android package and iOS bundle ID: com.scoutvela.blockisles

Stored on your device

Your best score, saved game, adventure progress, coins, themes, power-ups and settings stay on your phone.

Online scores (tournaments, leagues, friends)

To place you on the weekly tournament, league, daily challenge and friends boards, the game creates an anonymous account for you. We store a random player ID and friend code, the player name you choose (or a default "PlayerXXXX" name), your scores with the number of moves and game time, your league and trophies, your friends list, and players you block or report. There is no chat. We never ask for your email, phone number or real name. This data is hosted by Supabase on servers in the European Union and is encrypted in transit.

Player safety

Player names are filtered for offensive words. Tap any player on a leaderboard to report their name or block them. A name reported by several players is reset to a default name, and we review reports.

Deleting your data

Tap your profile (top left) → Delete online account to delete your online account, scores and friendships at once. Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your phone.

Crack the Rule

Android package and iOS bundle ID: com.scoutvela.cracktherule

Stored on your device

Your case progress, stars, coins, rulebook, daily case streak, language and settings stay on your phone.

Online scores (weekly board)

To place you on the weekly leaderboard, the game creates an anonymous account for you. We store a random player ID and code, the player name you choose (or a default name), the highest case you solved, your weekly points, the day of your last daily case, and players you block or report. There is no chat. We never ask for your email, phone number or real name. This data is hosted by Supabase on servers in the European Union and is encrypted in transit.

Player safety

Player names are filtered for offensive words. Tap any player on the weekly board to report their name or block them, and we review reports.

Deleting your data

Tap Settings (gear, top left) → Delete online data to delete your online account and weekly points at once. Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your phone.

Legion Rush

Android package: com.scoutvela.legionrush

Stored on your device

Your gold, weapons, upgrades, best distance, your own top 10 runs and settings stay on your phone.

Online rankings (world and weekly)

To place you on the world and weekly distance rankings, the game creates an anonymous account for you. We store a random player ID and code, the nickname you choose (or a default "CommanderXXXX" name), your best distance, your best distance this week, the number of runs you sent, and players you block or report. There is no chat. We never ask for your email, phone number or real name. This data is hosted by Supabase on servers in the European Union and is encrypted in transit.

Player safety

Nicknames are filtered for offensive words. Tap any player in the rankings to report their name or block them, and we review reports.

Deleting your data

Tap Settings (☰) → Player name & account → Delete online account to delete your online account and all your ranking entries at once. Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your phone.

Starword Atlas

Deleting your data

Advertising (all games)

Our games are free and show ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (Android advertising ID or Apple IDFA), approximate location from your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

  • On iPhone the game asks for your permission before using the IDFA (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are not personalised.
  • Players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland see a consent form on first launch and can change their choice later in the game's settings.

See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Children

The games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has used the online features of Donut Destroyer, Block Isles, Crack the Rule, Legion Rush or Starword Atlas, email us and we will delete the account.

Selling and sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers named on this page (Google AdMob and, for Donut Destroyer, Block Isles, Crack the Rule, Legion Rush and Starword Atlas, Supabase) so the games can work.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it. Most of this you can do yourself in the game; for anything else, email us with your player name or friend code.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
